🥣 The Singapore Bar-Model Recipe (works for almost everything):
  1. Draw one bar for the TOTAL (or the shape's full side/perimeter).
  2. Chop the bar into the parts you know + the part you don't (call it x or y).
  3. Compare the bars — what must be equal? What adds up?
  4. Turn the picture into one equation.
  5. Solve it like a recipe: undo additions/subtractions first, then multiplications.
  6. Check: put your answer back into the ORIGINAL numbers — do both sides taste the same?
