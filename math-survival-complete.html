🧮 Working Space

Line numbers up by place value — just like on paper. Pick +, −, ×, or ÷, type your number(s), tap Solve. For × and ÷, the second number is a single digit (0–9) for now — short multiplication and short/"bus-stop" division. It carries/borrows automatically — the little blue and pink notes above each column show exactly what changed, so it stays clear what the digits originally were.

💡 Pink = borrowing (subtraction): the column lent 10, so the column it borrowed from shows −1 and gets crossed out. Blue = carrying (addition/multiplication) or a "bring-down" remainder (division). This is the same "regroup ten" / "bring down" trick from primary school — just typed instead of handwritten.
🔢 BIDMAS / PEMDAS

Same order of operations, two different acronyms — UK textbooks say BIDMAS, US ones say PEMDAS:

1st
Brackets / Parentheses
2nd
Indices / Exponents — powers & roots
3rd
Divide & Multiply — left to right
4th
Add & Subtract — left to right
💡 Divide and Multiply are equal priority — just work left to right between them. Same for Add and Subtract.
